Fun Facts

  • Alternate Story: Early versions of the movie were almost completely different. One idea had Kraven turning New York into a giant trap for Spider-Man, while another had Peter leaving New York and dealing with someone impersonating him as Spider-Man.
  • Jackie Chan Connection: The film's fight sequences were coordinated by the Jackie Chan Stunt Team, which had previously worked with director Destin Daniel Cretton on Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings.
  • Filming in Scotland: Parts of New York City were actually filmed in Glasgow, Scotland. Locations including George Square and Merchant City were transformed to look like New York.
  • Spider-Man's Stretching Scene: Tom Holland came up with the idea for Spider-Man to stretch before a fight. The joke was inspired by Holland's own need to stretch during filming.
  • Different Spider-Man Suit: Brand New Day marked Holland's seventh time wearing a Spider-Man suit for a live-action Marvel movie.
  • Real Location for Punisher: The Historic Dockyard Chatham in England was used as Frank Castle's home and for other scenes involving the Punisher.
  • Michael Giacchino Returns: Michael Giacchino composed the film's music, continuing his connection with the MCU's Spider-Man after scoring Homecoming, Far From Home, and No Way Home.
